## Confirmation states
- **Confirmed-U**: explicit current owner decision (written source or recorded conversation).
- **Confirmed-S**: owner-supplied specification.
- **Confirmed-P**: binding workspace policy (CLAUDE.md, docs/STACK.md, ADRs).
- **Relayed**: owner preference relayed in conversation, no written source yet.
- **Proposed**: planner default; reversible; not owner-confirmed.
- **Conditional**: source marks it optional; needs a decision.
- **Open**: behavior needs an answer (Q-ID).
- **Future**: confirmed as outside the MVP, recorded for a later phase.
- **Removed**: removed from scope; kept for history.
